## The Gap

Test-time compute scaling has become the predominant paradigm for boosting LLM accuracy at inference time. Prevailing scaling laws quantify how performance improves as more forward passes are spent on repeated sampling, majority voting (self-consistency), or verifier search. However, virtually all existing work operates on a flawed foundational assumption: **they query the model repeatedly on a single, fixed prompt**, treating decoding randomness as the sole dimension of test-time variation.

In reality, most reasoning problems possess rich **algebraic symmetry groups** ($G$):
- A graph's isomorphism class is invariant to node permutations ($S_n$).
- A program's semantic execution is invariant to variable renaming ($\alpha$-conversion).
- A multiple-choice problem is invariant to option reordering ($S_k$).
- Deductive reasoning is invariant to premise order.

Because frozen LLMs lack built-in geometric equivariance, their answers fluctuate wildly across semantically equivalent rewritings. Standard test-time compute explores only the vertical "decoding fiber" above a single representation $e \cdot x$, leaving the horizontal "orbit" $G \cdot x$ completely unobserved.

## The Increment

**One sentence**: By proving that decoding randomness and algebraic rewriting are fundamentally non-interchangeable, this paper establishes a model-agnostic test-time scaling law showing that just $\mathcal{O}(\log |G|)$ random group rewritings suffice to approximate the full invariant projection $P_G$, dramatically outperforming repeated prompt sampling at matched compute.

### Core Mechanism

The framework, termed **Model-Agnostic Symmetrization**, formalizes inference over transformation groups:

1. **Orbit Projection vs. Decoding Fibers**:
   Let $G$ be a finite symmetry group acting on prompt space $\mathcal{X}$, and let $y^\star(x)$ be the invariant ground truth ($y^\star(g \cdot x) = y^\star(x)$). When queried on rewriting $g \cdot x$, a frozen model produces response representations $V_x(g, \omega) \in \mathcal{Z}$ with expectation $F_x(g) = \mathbb{E}_{\omega}[V_x(g, \omega)]$.
   The ideal invariant decision is given by the orthogonal group projection $P_G$:
   $$(P_G F_x)(h) = \frac{1}{|G|} \sum_{g \in G} F_x(gh)$$
   This projection extracts the consensus across the entire orbit $G \cdot x$, stripping prompt-formatting bias while preserving discriminatory power across answer classes.
2. **The No-Go Theorem (Theorem 1)**:
   The authors prove that any inference algorithm that only samples traces from the canonical prompt $e \cdot x$ (regardless of how many thousands of tokens or temperature passes it spends) incurs an expected error of at least $1/2$ whenever the true orbit average differs from the local response. Decoding exploration cannot substitute for orbit exploration.
3. **The Logarithmic Sparse Rewriting Law (Theorem 2 & 3)**:
   Exhaustively evaluating all $|G|$ rewritings is computationally impossible (e.g., a 20-node graph has $20! \approx 2.4 \times 10^{18}$ permutations). The authors prove using operator concentration that a randomly drawn multiset $S \subset G$ of size:
   $$|S| = \mathcal{O}\left( \frac{\log(|G| / \delta)}{\epsilon^2} \right)$$
   guarantees that the empirical average $A_S F = \frac{1}{|S|}\sum_{g \in S} F(gh)$ satisfies $\|A_S F - P_G F\| \le \epsilon$ with probability $1 - \delta$.
   Crucially, this logarithmic bound holds **uniformly** across all models, all decoding temperatures, and all post-hoc aggregation rules (majority voting, logit averaging, verifier scoring).

**Structural Metaphor**: Imagine an art committee trying to appraise a complex 3D crystal sculpture sealed in a display room. Standard self-consistency is like hiring 16 inspectors who all stand at the exact same North window, taking 16 photographs from the identical angle and arguing over lighting artifacts (sampling along the decoding fiber). Test-time symmetrization realizes that the sculpture has rotational symmetry $G$. Instead of wasting 16 shots at the North window, it sends 4 inspectors to the North, East, South, and West windows ($|S| = \mathcal{O}(\log |G|)$). ### Key Concepts

- **Decoding Fiber vs. Group Orbit**: In the geometry of prompt space, the decoding fiber represents the stochastic randomness of token generation at a fixed input prompt, while the group orbit represents the set of all semantically equivalent re-encodings of the underlying problem.
- **Model-Agnostic Symmetrization**: Imposing exact algebraic symmetry on frozen, off-the-shelf foundation models at inference time via pre-sampling and post-aggregation, without modifying network weights or requiring equivariant layers.
- **Logarithmic Compression of Orbits**: The mathematical property that an exponential transformation space of size $|G|$ can be represented to high fidelity by only $\mathcal{O}(\log |G|)$ uniformly sampled elements, analogous to Johnson-Lindenstrauss random projections in metric spaces.

## Expert Assessment

- **Problem choice**: Exceptional foundational significance. Everyone in AI is talking about spending test-time compute, but almost no one was studying *where in prompt space* that compute should be allocated. Proving that symmetry rewritings beat repeated sampling is a seminal contribution.
- **Method maturity**: Pure mathematical elegance. The operator-theoretic proofs in Hilbert spaces provide tight, universal bounds that hold simultaneously across models and decoding strategies. A practical constraint is that the user must know or be able to compute the symmetry group $G$ of the task (e.g., node permutations or graph automorphisms); for open-ended creative tasks where the symmetry group is ill-defined, model-agnostic symmetrization cannot be readily formalized.
- **Experimental integrity**: Thorough and convincing. Tested across four prominent open-weight models (Qwen2.5-7B, Phi-4-mini, Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen2.5-14B) on graph algorithms, propositional logic, and permutation-sensitive QA. The empirical accuracy curves match the theoretical $\mathcal{O}(\log |G|)$ scaling exponents with remarkable fidelity.
- **Writing quality**: Flawless mathematical exposition, clean notation, and crisp conceptual separation of orbits versus fibers.
- **Verdict**: **Strong Accept**. A cornerstone paper that will permanently reshape the theory of test-time compute scaling laws.

## Takeaways

- **Never spend all test-time passes on a single prompt**: If your reasoning task has any permutation, renaming, or reordering symmetry, allocate forward passes across $\mathcal{O}(\log |G|)$ rewritings instead of sampling the original prompt 16 times.
- **You only need logarithmically many rewritings**: For a group of size $10^{18}$, you do not need millions of rewritings; 30 to 50 random group samples capture virtually the entire invariant projection.
- **Symmetry can be retrofitted without retraining**: You do not need to train expensive equivariant graph neural networks; standard frozen LLMs achieve geometric invariance through test-time symmetrization wrappers.

## 缺口

以思考链扩展、多数投票（Self-Consistency）与验证器搜索为代表的“测试期算力缩放”（Test-Time Compute Scaling），已成为释放大模型推理能力的主流范式。
现有的算力缩放定律（Scaling Laws）无一例外遵循一个未经深究的底层假设：**它们在测试期对同一个固定的 Prompt 进行多次独立解码采样**，将模型生成的不确定性视为推理测试期算力的唯一来源。

然而在现实物理与数理世界中，绝大多数复杂推理任务天然具备深厚的**代数对称群（Symmetry Group $G$）**：
- 图结构对节点的重命名与拓扑排序完全不变（置换群 $S_n$）；
- 程序代码的功能语义对局部变量名重命名完全不变（$\alpha$-等价）；
- 多项选择题与逻辑推理对选项排列和前提假设的陈述顺序完全不变。

由于冻结参数的通用大语言模型（Frozen LLMs）内部缺乏几何等变性架构，模型在面对语义完全相同的不同文本重写时，预测结果往往剧烈震荡。
现有的测试期采样仅仅在单一固定提示词对应的“垂直解码纤维”上反复打转，而对语义完全等价的“水平群轨道”（Group Orbit）视而不见。

## 增量

**一句话**: 本文从理论上证明解码端反复重采样在本质上无法替代输入端代数重写，并推导出严格的模型无关缩放法则：仅需 $\mathcal{O}(\log |G|)$ 次随机群重写即可高精度逼近全对称轨道投影，在同等算力消耗下显著消除格式偏置并大幅提升推理准确率。

### 核心机制

作者构建了名为**模型无关对称化（Model-Agnostic Symmetrization）**的形式化理论体系：

1. **群轨道与解码纤维的几何分离**：
   设有限群 $G$ 作用于提示词空间 $\mathcal{X}$，目标函数具备群不变性 $y^\star(g \cdot x) = y^\star(x)$。
   大模型在重写输入 $g \cdot x$ 上的输出表征记为 $V_x(g, \omega)$，其期望响应场为 $F_x(g) = \mathbb{E}_\omega[V_x(g, \omega)]$。
   全群平均算子 $P_G$ 定义为将函数正交投影到常数不变子空间的正交投影：
   $$(P_G F_x)(h) = \frac{1}{|G|} \sum_{g \in G} F_x(gh)$$
   该投影能彻底洗刷掉模型对特定提示词排版与符号命名的偶然偏见，同时完整保留用于区分正确答案的核心决策信息。
2. **重采样不可能性定理（Theorem 1）**：
   作者给出了令人震撼的否定性结论：任何仅从原初提示词 $e \cdot x$ 抽取解码样本的算法（不论采样多少次、采用何种温度或搜索策略），必然存在两个在单一提示词下行为完全相同但在全群轨道上均值截然相反的可能世界，算法在最坏情况下的错误率至少为 $1/2$。
   解码随机性永远无法穿透模型在未见重写状态下的系统性盲区。
3. **对数级稀疏重写缩放律（Theorem 2 & 3）**：
   在 20 个节点的图上遍历所有置换需要 $|G| = 20! \approx 2.4 \times 10^{18}$ 次前向传播，计算上绝不可行。
   作者基于 Hilbert 空间算子集中不等式严格证明：随机均匀抽取的重写集合 $S \subset G$，只要规模满足：
   $$|S| = \mathcal{O}\left( \frac{\log(|G| / \delta)}{\epsilon^2} \right)$$
   就能以至少 $1 - \delta$ 的概率保证经验均值 $A_S F$ 与理论全群投影 $P_G F$ 的均方误差不超过 $\epsilon$。
   更为关键的是，**该对数界具有通用性（Model-Agnostic）**——它独立于具体的神经网络权重、解码规则（多数表决、Logit 平均、验证器打分）与温度设置，抽取的这组重写样本可在所有模型上无损复用。

```
全对称群轨道 G * x (以 20 节点图为例，|G| = 20! ~ 2.4 x 10^18 种排列)
  [ 节点排列 1 ]    [ 节点排列 2 ]    [ 节点排列 3 ]  ...  [ 节点排列 |G| ]
        \                 |                 /                 /
         \                |                /                 /
     [ 稀疏子集均匀采样: 仅需 |S| = O(log |G|) ~ 40 次前向传播 ]
                                  |
                                  v
              [ 跨模型通用的重写提示词集合 S ]
                                  |
         +------------------------+------------------------+
         |                                                 |
         v                                                 v
    输入重写 1: g1*x                                  输入重写 k: gk*x
  (如: 变量名序列置换 A)                            (如: 变量名序列置换 K)
         |                                                 |
         v                                                 v
   各自执行常规解码                                  各自执行常规解码
   omega_1 ~ p( . | g1*x)                            omega_k ~ p( . | gk*x)
         \                                                 /
          \                                               /
           v                                             v
       [ 不变性正交聚合: A_S F = (1/|S|) * Sum V(g_i, omega_i) ]
```

**核喻（多角度全景鉴宝法）**：
将整个过程比作**考古鉴定委员会对一件放置在展厅中央的多面体古董进行断代鉴定**。
传统的测试期多数投票（Self-Consistency）就像派了 16 位鉴定师，全部挤在正北方向唯一的窗户前（固定原 Prompt 纤维），从完全相同的角度拍摄了 16 张照片，激烈争吵反光阴影是真纹理还是划痕。
而模型无关对称化认识到这件古董具备三维旋转对称性 $G$。
它绝不在北窗浪费 16 次快门，而是让 4 位摄影师分别站在东、西、南、北四个正交窗口（对数级稀疏重写 $|S| = \mathcal{O}(\log |G|)$）。
通过对四个视角投影的多重轮廓进行交叉比对，任何单重视角下的光学幻影瞬间消解，委员会以极低的拍摄成本完美还原出古董的几何真容。

### 关键概念

- **解码纤维与群轨道（Decoding Fiber vs. Group Orbit）**：几何深度学习视角下的提示词空间拓扑。
解码纤维指在单一确定文本输入下由于自回归采样引入的随机漫步；群轨道指由代数变换群生成的语义完全同构的输入空间流形。
- **模型无关对称化（Model-Agnostic Symmetrization）**：一种无需重新训练模型参数、无需设计专用等变网络架构，仅在测试期通过外部预处理重写与后置代数聚合强制赋予大模型群不变性的理论框架。
- **群轨道的对数压缩（Logarithmic Orbit Compression）**：指庞大到指数级维度的代数对称变换空间，其统计方差可被对数尺度的有限随机观测点完全吸收，本质是高维几何测度集中现象在离散群论上的优美投射。

## 框架转变

```
传统方案（非结构化测试期算力缩放）：
  提示词 x ---> 在 x 上无脑重复采样 16 次 ---> 多数投票表决
  [准确率存在天花板：模型对表达形式 x 本身存在的固有偏置和盲点无法消除]

本文方案（模型无关测试期对称化）：
  提示词 x ---> 随机生成 4 种群代数重写 {g1*x, g2*x, g3*x, g4*x} ---> 各采样 1 次 ---> 轨道平均
  [同样消耗 4 次前向传播算力，但通过对消表示偏置，图与逻辑推理准确率净增 15-30%]
```

一句话：从在单一提示词上堆砌冗余采样，转向沿着代数对称群轨道分配对数级的输入重写算力，核心转变在于用任务的内生几何对称性消除表示诱导的系统性幻觉。
