**Paper**: [2611.27443](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27443) — Learning When to Ask: Advisor-Aware Post-Training for SLM Agents\
**Authors**: Aasheesh Singh, Vishal Vaddina\
**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI\
**Reading scope**: Original arXiv v1 HTML main text and all five appendices, including the training recipe, baselines, reported results, budgets and advisor swaps. Tables and figure captions were read; graphics were not visually inspected. The experiments were not reproduced.

## The Problem

Imagine a small agent moving an object through a household task. It has to find the object, clean it and put it in the right place. A stronger advisor can suggest a plan, but the small agent still issues every command. Asking repeatedly may waste money; asking only near the action limit can leave too little room to carry out the plan. This is an illustrative situation in the paper's household-control setting.

Aasheesh Singh and Vishal Vaddina study this division of labor. Their advisor reads the full trajectory and returns strategic guidance as a tool observation. It never acts on the environment. The worker must decide when to ask and then translate the answer into its own actions (§3.1).

Their opening tests suggest that attaching an advisor is insufficient for the studied agents. Some barely ask; others ask without converting the help into progress. The proposed ADAPT recipe trains advice-seeking as a capability inside the smaller model, rather than leaving the whole job to a prompt (§1).

## The Explanation

### Teach the worker to use the help it can already request

ADAPT starts with a supervised warm-start. The authors collect the small model's own trajectories, retain successful episodes, and train on a mixture of successes with and without advice. The model practices competent independent actions alongside well-formed requests. The success labels come from the environment, without a learned reward model or human annotation (§3.2).

Successful traces alone do not tell the model whether a particular call was worthwhile. The next stage therefore pauses a trajectory at selected steps and compares two continuations: one asks the advisor at that point, the other acts directly. Each branch is rolled out repeatedly to the final task outcome. This measures the benefit of asking now, rather than judging whether the request sounds sensible (§3.3).

The resulting training pairs teach three things. **Ask** pairs favor help when it clearly improves the chance of success. **Abstain** pairs favor independent action when the worker already succeeds and advice adds nothing. **Follow** pairs give both branches the same advice, then prefer the continuation that acts on it successfully over one that drifts to failure.

That last distinction matters in the household example. Receiving a plan does not complete the cleaning or placement actions. ADAPT trains Follow pairs over the subsequent sequence of actions, while Ask and Abstain pairs train the immediate decision. Advice remains an observation; its words are not treated as the worker's own action tokens (§3.3; Appendix A).

### Put a price on asking, with an average budget

Preference training can still produce too many calls. ADAPT's final stage rewards task completion and charges a price for each advisor request. If the training batch's average call count exceeds the chosen budget, the price rises; if it falls below, the price can decrease. The model is also anchored to its previous trained behavior (§3.4).

The formal constraint controls **expected calls per episode**. It is not a hard cap on every individual task, and it budgets call counts rather than advisor tokens or dollars. In the reported household setting, a budget of two leads to an observed mean of **1.09 calls per episode**. Guidance does not consume a native environment step, but it still incurs model usage and must leave useful actions to perform (§§3.1, 3.4 and 4.1; Appendix B).

### What the reported results establish

The trained worker is Qwen3-8B, with Gemini 3.7 Flash as the advisor. The study's household harness, called TextWorld, uses ALFWorld tasks. The other test sets cover code generation with test feedback and text-only web research. Training and test data are separated, with **109 household tasks, 134 coding problems and 103 research questions** in the respective test sets (Appendix C).

The untrained comparison below already has the same advisor tool under the Neutral prompt. The reported ADAPT results therefore compare post-training with an existing advised worker, not with an agent denied access to help (§5; Table 2).

| Test setting | Untrained advised worker | ADAPT | ADAPT mean advisor calls |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Household control | 24.5% | 77.32% | 1.09 |
| LiveCodeBench-stdin | 21.8% | 56.01% | 1.02 |
| GAIA-Text | 17.5% | 49.25% | 2.12 |

Prompting helps unevenly. The Nudged prompt raises the coding result to **35.1%**, while household and research results reach **28.2%** and **19.4%**. In these tests, training adds more than the supplied prompting change. That does not establish that every possible prompt would behave the same (§5).

The coding exception is important. Another trained baseline, AgentRL, reaches **58.3%**, above ADAPT's **56.01%**. Both exceed the reported hindsight Oracle score of **52%**, because that diagnostic uses the base worker with improved asking decisions. It is not a ceiling on what a newly trained worker can achieve. The paper interprets the coding result as evidence that learning to use test feedback and repair code can matter alongside asking for advice (§§4.2 and 5).

### A different advisor can still be useful

The authors replace the training advisor with two unseen alternatives. Household success is reported at **79%** with Claude Opus 4.8 and **68%** with DeepSeek V4 Flash, compared with about **77.3%** using Gemini. This supports transfer to those tested advisors, with a qualification: the swaps are single-seed evaluations, while the main results average three seeds (Appendix D; Table 5).

The worker remains responsible for converting guidance into action. The transfer test therefore asks whether the learned interaction survives a changed source of advice, rather than handing the task to that source.

## Core Concepts

**Advice and control.** The larger model offers a plan; the smaller model still executes. Evaluating the final trajectory checks whether the advice became useful behavior.

**The value of asking now.** Compare asking and acting from the same selected state, then look at subsequent success. A well-written request alone does not establish that it improved the task.

**An average-call budget.** Training adjusts the price to control expected usage. A strict per-task cap would require a separate enforcement rule; variable advice length also means equal call counts need not imply equal spending.

## The Insight

Useful help-seeking combines a worthwhile moment, successful follow-through and an appropriate bill. The future trajectory is the test of whether guidance produced progress. ADAPT makes these responsibilities explicit in its training recipe.

## A Critical Reading

Some reporting needs clarification. Table 2 gives the untrained coding worker **0.06 calls per episode** while saying **9% of episodes ask**. Under the definitions in §4.1, that 9% alone requires at least 0.09 calls per episode, since each asking episode contains at least one call. The values are not mutually consistent as labeled. The success rates above are reported findings, not independently verified measurements.

Figure 2 also requires restraint: its caption explicitly says that only the budget-two operating point is measured. The budget-one and budget-four points are illustrations left for future work. They cannot establish a measured success–cost curve. The formal average-budget constraint should likewise be distinguished from the prose's suggestion of a per-episode maximum (Appendix B; §3.4).

The comparisons are informative but not a clean removal of one stage at a time. Step-DPO uses one-step updates for Follow pairs, while ADAPT trains the continuation; AgentRL starts from the same supervised checkpoint but uses a fixed asking price. Their differences do not by themselves isolate every component's causal contribution (§4.2).

Finally, ADAPT is trained on one open-weight model using low-rank weight updates and modest task-specific data. The outcome-supervised recipe fits environments with checkable success signals, and the advisor-swap evidence is limited to two single-seed tests. Broader models, subjective tasks and the full cost of generating training rollouts remain outside the demonstrated result (§6; Appendices C–D).

## What You Can Use

When equipping a small worker with an advisor, evaluate where it asks, how it follows the guidance and what it ultimately finishes. Track mean call counts alongside the fraction of tasks that ask and actual spend, checking that the definitions agree. For the household worker, a useful plan still has to become successful commands within the action budget. The recipe offers a way to train that behavior; its reported gains and cost claims should be read with the evaluation limits intact.

**阅读范围**：阅读 arXiv v1 原始 HTML 正文与五个附录，包括训练方法、基线、报告结果、求助预算和顾问替换。

## 问题

设想一个小模型助手做家居任务：找到物品，清洁，再放到指定位置。
大模型顾问可以给计划，命令仍由小助手自己发出。
不停问，可能白花钱；等动作额度快用完才问，又可能来不及把计划做完。
这是帮助理解的场景，贴近论文的家居控制设置。

Aasheesh Singh 与 Vishal Vaddina 研究这份分工。
顾问能读到完整过程，把战略建议作为工具返回值交给助手，却不会直接操作环境。
小助手得决定何时问，再把建议变成自己的动作，见第 3.1 节。

开头的测试显示，对这些模型和任务，接上顾问还不够。
有的几乎不问，有的问了也没形成进展。
ADAPT 因此把求助作为小模型内部要训练的能力，而不把整件事留给一段提示词，见第 1 节。

## 翻译

### 先让助手会用已经能问到的帮助

ADAPT 先做一次监督训练。
作者收集小模型自己的执行过程，保留成功任务，混合有求助与没有求助的成功轨迹。
模型同时练习独立动作与格式正确的请求；标签来自环境成败，不另训奖励模型，也不用人工标注，见第 3.2 节。

成功轨迹本身，还不能说明某一次求助值得不值得。
下一阶段会在选定步骤暂停，比较两条续做：一条此刻问顾问，一条直接行动。
每条分支重复跑到任务结束，再按最终成败比较。
量的是「现在问能带来多少帮助」，不是请求写得像不像好问题，见第 3.3 节。

由此得到三类训练对照。
**求助**：如果问了明显更容易完成，就优先学问。
**不求助**：如果本来就能做成，问也没增加收益，就优先直接做。
**执行建议**：两条分支收到同样建议，优先选择照建议做成的后续过程，而不是后来偏离、最终失败的过程。

第三类对家居例子尤其重要。
收到清洁和摆放的计划，物品还没有因此被清洁和摆好。
ADAPT 对执行建议的样本训练后续整段动作，对求助与不求助的样本训练眼前决定。
顾问说的话属于观察，不当成小助手自己输出的动作词元，见第 3.3 节及附录 A。

### 给求助定价，控制平均用量

偏好训练仍可能让模型问得太多。
最后一阶段奖励任务完成，再对每次求助收取一个训练中的价格。
一批任务的平均求助数高于所选预算，就提高价格；低于预算，价格可以下降。
更新还会锚定先前学好的策略，见第 3.4 节。

形式上的限制控制的是**每个任务的期望求助次数**。
它不是每项任务都不得超出的硬上限，预算单位也是调用次数，而不是顾问词元或美元。
论文的家居设置选预算二，报告的实际平均为 **每项任务 1.09 次**。
求助不消耗原生环境动作步骤，却仍要付模型用量，也得给执行计划留下动作空间，见第 3.1、3.4、4.1 节及附录 B。

### 报告的结果支持到哪里

被训练的小助手是 Qwen3-8B，顾问是 Gemini 3.7 Flash。
论文把基于 ALFWorld 的家居控制环境称为 TextWorld；另外两组测试是带测试反馈的代码生成，以及纯文本网页研究。
训练与测试分开，三个测试集分别有 **109 项家居任务、134 道代码题、103 道研究问题**，见附录 C。

下表的未训练对照，已经在中性提示词下接上同一个顾问工具。
比较的是后训练与已有顾问的小助手，不是把能求助与不能求助混在一起，见第 5 节及表 2。

| 测试设置 | 未训练、已接顾问 | ADAPT | ADAPT 平均求助数 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 家居控制 | 24.5% | 77.32% | 1.09 |
| LiveCodeBench 标准输入输出子集 | 21.8% | 56.01% | 1.02 |
| GAIA 纯文本子集 | 17.5% | 49.25% | 2.12 |

提示词的作用并不一致。
额外提醒何时考虑求助，代码成功率升到 **35.1%**，家居和研究分别为 **28.2%**、**19.4%**。
在这些测试里，训练的增益比所用提醒更大，不能据此断言所有可能的提示词都会一样，见第 5 节。

代码任务的例外必须留下。
另一条训练基线 AgentRL 达到 **58.3%**，高于 ADAPT 的 **56.01%**。
两者还都超过报告的事后 Oracle 对照 **52%**，因为后者使用基础助手，只改善求助决定，不是重新训练过的助手所能达到的上限。
作者把这项结果解释为：学习利用测试反馈、修正代码，也能与何时求助一起影响表现，见第 4.2、5 节。

### 换一个顾问，帮助还可能用得上

作者用两个训练时未见的顾问替换原顾问。
家居成功率换成 Claude Opus 4.8 后报告为 **79%**，换 DeepSeek V4 Flash 为 **68%**，原 Gemini 配置约 **77.3%**。
它支持向这两个顾问迁移，但替换实验只跑单个随机种子，主结果取三个种子的平均，见附录 D 及表 5。

负责把指导变成动作的，始终是小助手。
所以这里检验的是已学会的互动能否接住不同建议来源，而不是把任务交给顾问代办。

## 核心概念

**建议与控制。** 大模型给计划，小模型发动作。
看最后的执行轨迹，才能知道建议有没有成为有用行为。

**此刻求助的价值。** 从同一个选定状态分别求助和直接做，再比较后面的成败。
请求写得好，本身不能证明它帮助了任务。

**平均调用预算。** 训练调价格，以控制期望用量。
每项任务的硬上限还要另设执行规则；建议长短不同，相同次数也未必花同样的钱。

## 洞见

有效求助要接起三个环节：值得问的时刻、能做成的后续动作，以及合适的花费。
后续轨迹才是建议有没有变成进展的检验。
ADAPT 把这份责任写进了训练方法。

## 博导审稿

部分报告口径需要澄清。
表 2 写未训练代码助手平均每项任务 **0.06 次求助**，同时又说 **9% 的任务会求助**。
按第 4.1 节的定义，仅这 9% 就至少贡献平均 0.09 次，因为每项求助任务至少有一次调用。
这两个数按现有标签不能同时成立；上面的成功率是论文报告值，不是独立核验过的测量。

图 2 也不能多读。
图注明确说，只有预算二的工作点是测量结果，预算一与预算四是留待未来实验的示意。
它们不能建立实测的成功率与成本曲线。
形式上的平均预算限制，也要与正文所说的每项任务最大次数分开，见附录 B 及第 3.4 节。

基线有信息量，却不是每次干净地只去掉一个阶段。
Step-DPO 对执行建议只训练下一步，ADAPT 训练整个后续过程；AgentRL 从同一个监督检查点开始，但用固定求助价格。
仅凭这些差别，还不能单独量出每个零件的因果贡献，见第 4.2 节。

最后，ADAPT 只在一种开放权重模型上，用低秩权重更新和有限任务数据训练。
方法适合有可检查成败信号的环境，换顾问的证据也限于两个单种子测试。
更多模型、主观任务，以及生成训练轨迹的完整成本，都还超出已展示的结果，见第 6 节及附录 C—D。

## 启发

给小助手接顾问时，要一起评价何时问、怎样执行建议，以及最后完成了什么。
把平均次数、会求助的任务比例和实际花费一起记录，并检查定义与数值是否对得上。
回到家居助手，好计划仍要在动作额度内变成成功命令。
这套方法给出训练行为的办法；报告的增益与成本，需要连着评估边界一起读。
