## The Gap

The emergence of test-time scaling in reasoning models (such as o1, DeepSeek-R1, and Qwen-QwQ) demonstrated that allocating extended chain-of-thought (CoT) tokens unlocks superhuman performance on graduate-level mathematics and competitive programming. However, this power comes with a severe economic drawback: **compulsive over-thinking**.

Careful behavioral audits reveal that models often solve the problem in the first 30% to 50% of their reasoning budget, but spend thousands of subsequent tokens repeatedly re-verifying trivial arithmetic steps, muttering "Wait, let me double check from the beginning...", and second-guessing established proofs.

Existing approaches to shorten CoT suffer from unacceptable compromises:
1. **Supervised Distillation / SFT**: Requires retraining each specific model version, which often degrades out-of-distribution reasoning and calibration.
2. **Hard Heuristics & Token Bans**: Banning hesitation markers like "Wait" or truncating tokens after an arbitrary budget frequently cuts the model off right before catching legitimate calculation bugs, causing accuracy to collapse.

```
   Problem: "Solve 7x^2 - 14x = 0"
   Model CoT Generation:
   Tokens 1-100: "Factor 7x: 7x(x - 2) = 0. Therefore x = 0 or x = 2." [SOLVED!]

   Tokens 101-900 (Compulsive Over-Verification Loop):
   "Wait, let me re-evaluate... What if x is a complex number? Let's check with quadratic formula...
    -b +- sqrt(b^2 - 4ac)... (-(-14) +- sqrt(196 - 0)) / 14... 14/14 = 1 +- 1... 0 and 2.
    Wait, could there be a division by zero error? Let me rethink..."
         |
```

## The Increment

**One sentence**: DoubtLess establishes a training-free contrastive decoding framework where a lightweight reasoning model computes the difference between "brief" and "verbose" logit distributions over the ongoing reasoning trace, steering a 32B model to eliminate obsessive redundant checking and saving 31% of thinking tokens with zero accuracy degradation.

### Core Mechanism

DoubtLess operates entirely at inference time via an asymmetric dual-prompter setup using a small model:

1. **The Brief vs. Verbose Thinker Setup**:
   - Let $\mathcal{M}_{\text{large}}$ be the primary generation model (e.g., Qwen3-32B), and $\mathcal{M}_{\text{small}}$ be a fast, lightweight reasoning checkpoint (e.g., a 1.5B or 3B model).
   - At each generation step $t$, the small model reads the ongoing chain-of-thought prefix twice under two distinct system instructions:
     - Prompt 1 ($\mathcal{P}_{\text{brief}}$): Instructs the model to be extremely concise, skip routine re-examinations, and proceed directly to conclusion.
     - Prompt 2 ($\mathcal{P}_{\text{verbose}}$): Instructs the model to engage in exhaustive, verbose verification.
2. **Logit-Level Steering Intervention**:
   - The small model produces two logit vectors over the next token: $\mathbf{z}_{\text{small}}^{\text{brief}}$ and $\mathbf{z}_{\text{small}}^{\text{verbose}}$.
   - The directional steering delta represents the pure direction of brevity:
     $$\Delta \mathbf{z} = \mathbf{z}_{\text{small}}^{\text{brief}} - \mathbf{z}_{\text{small}}^{\text{verbose}}$$
   - This delta is scaled by a strength coefficient $\alpha$ and added directly into the primary model's unnormalized logits:
     $$\mathbf{z}_{\text{final}} = \mathbf{z}_{\text{large}} + \alpha \cdot \Delta \mathbf{z}$$
3. **Surgical Pruning After Truth Emergence**:
   - Behavioral analysis confirms that DoubtLess does not suppress essential early exploratory math. Instead, the steering delta cleanly penalizes self-doubting tokens ("Wait", "Let me retry", "Hold on") *specifically after* the correct mathematical answer has already surfaced in the latent context.

```

   [Small Model: Prompt "Brief"]           [Small Model: Prompt "Verbose"]
   Logits: z_small^brief                   Logits: z_small^verbose

            Directly emits finalized answer! (-31% Tokens)
```

### Key Concepts

- **Contrastive Decoding**: A decoding-time intervention where logit distributions under conflicting prompts or models are subtracted to isolate a desired latent behavior without retraining.
- **Compulsive Over-Verification**: The specific failure mode in RLVR reasoning models where policy optimization over-rewards hesitation words, prompting the model to loop redundantly.
- **Asymmetric Model Steering**: Using a much smaller, cheaper model to calculate directional logit updates for a much larger, capable generation model.

## 缺口

测试阶段算力扩展（Test-Time Scaling）在 o1、DeepSeek-R1 与 QwQ 等前沿推理模型中的成功，证明了让模型自发展开数千甚至数万 Token 的长思维链（Chain-of-Thought, CoT），能够极大地激发大模型在高等数学与复杂算法竞赛中的解题潜力。
然而，这一突破伴随着极其高昂的商业推理成本：**强迫性过度反思（Compulsive Over-Thinking）**。

详尽的行为学轨迹审计揭示了一个惊人的现状：推理模型通常在消耗掉前 30% 到 50% 的思维链长度时，就已经推导出了完全正确的最终答案。然而在接下来的几千个 Token 里，模型却极易陷入某种强迫症式的重复验算死循环——反复在心里打鼓：「等等，让我从头再核对一遍...」、「如果这个参数为负数会怎样？」、「再用另一种方法反证一下...」。

现有的缩短思维链方案均存在严重的妥协：
1. **重新微调或蒸馏（SFT / RL 长度惩罚）**：成本昂贵，且极易破坏模型原本脆弱的长程推理能力与校准分布。
2. **硬性规则与禁用词截断**：简单粗暴地封杀「Wait」等反思词或强制限制长度，往往会在模型真正需要纠偏关键错误时将其直接掐死，导致正确率断崖式暴跌。

### 核心机制

DoubtLess 纯粹在推理阶段通过小模型作为「方向引航员」实施非对称干预：

1. **精炼思考者 vs. 啰嗦思考者（双角色对比）**：
   - 设主生成模型为 $\mathcal{M}_{\text{large}}$（如 Qwen3-32B），同时配备一个极其轻量快速的小型推理模型 $\mathcal{M}_{\text{small}}$（如 1.5B 或 3B 级别）。
   - 在主模型生成的每一个时间步 $t$，小模型同时读取当前已生成的长思维链历史，但在不同系统 Prompt 下分别前向计算：
     - Prompt 1（$\mathcal{P}_{\text{brief}}$，精炼指令）：要求言简意赅，完成关键推演即止，跳过平庸琐碎的从头验算。
     - Prompt 2（$\mathcal{P}_{\text{verbose}}$，啰嗦指令）：要求穷尽细节，极度保守地进行无休止的自我反思。
2. **Logit 级差分引导（Logit-Level Steering）**：
   - 小模型产出两组关于下一个 Token 的未归一化对数概率向量：$\mathbf{z}_{\text{small}}^{\text{brief}}$ 与 $\mathbf{z}_{\text{small}}^{\text{verbose}}$。
   - 二者的差值向量直接表征了纯粹的「精炼去冗余」方向：
     $$\Delta \mathbf{z} = \mathbf{z}_{\text{small}}^{\text{brief}} - \mathbf{z}_{\text{small}}^{\text{verbose}}$$
   - 将该差分向量按强度系数 $\alpha$ 叠加至主模型的原始 Logits 上完成最终采样：
     $$\mathbf{z}_{\text{final}} = \mathbf{z}_{\text{large}} + \alpha \cdot \Delta \mathbf{z}$$
3. **正确答案浮现后的外科手术式精准修剪**：
   - 深入的语义消融分析表明：DoubtLess 并没有压制前期真正解题所需的试错与探索；其引导向量在「正确答案首次出现」之后表现出极强的针对性，精准压制了「Wait」、「Let me rethink」等诱发死循环的犹豫词，催促模型迅速给出终局输出。

### 关键概念

- **对比解码（Contrastive Decoding）**：一种无需训练的解码时优化算法，通过计算模型在不同提示词或不同条件下的概率分布差值，增强特定期望特性（如精炼度、事实性）。
- **强迫性过度反思（Compulsive Over-Verification）**：强化学习可验证奖励（RLVR）模型中常见的一种副反应：模型过度将犹豫与反思词与奖励挂钩，导致即使算出答案依然不断重复推演。
- **非对称模型引导（Asymmetric Model Steering）**：利用低成本、低延迟的袖珍模型产生的 Logit 向量，去精准牵引大规模昂贵主模型生成方向的技术范式。
