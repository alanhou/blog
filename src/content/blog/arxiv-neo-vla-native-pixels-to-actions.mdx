## The Gap

The modern Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation model paradigm—championed by OpenVLA, Octo, and $\pi_0$—is built upon an implicit architectural assumption: **raw visual perception is first compressed by a frozen, separately pretrained vision encoder (e.g., CLIP, SigLIP, or DINOv2), and policy reasoning begins only after this visual endpoint**.

However, robotic manipulation differs fundamentally from internet-scale image captioning or classification:
1. **The Representation Mismatch**: Vision encoders trained on web images are optimized for semantic invariance (e.g., recognizing that a "mug" is a mug regardless of lighting, precise angle, or camera distance). Conversely, dexterous manipulation hinges precisely on fine-grained spatial signals: metric depth, edge contact geometry, micro-slips, and millimeter-level end-effector alignments.
2. **The Final-Layer Abstraction Trap**: Pretrained vision encoders progressively discard high-frequency spatial and geometric details in their deepest layers to maximize semantic abstraction. When a VLA model connects to this final layer, the geometric cues required for physical control have already been erased.
3. **Architectural Rigidity**: Encoder-based VLAs treat visual features as an immutable prefix. Scaling the language backbone or tuning the action head cannot recover high-resolution geometric signals lost upstream.

```
                      THE CONVENTIONAL VLA BOTTLENECK

  Raw Camera Pixels
        │
        ▼
  [ Pretrained Vision Encoder (CLIP / SigLIP) ]
  Layer 1 ──> Layer 6 ──> Layer 12 ──> Layer 24 (Final Layer)
  (Rich 3D Geometry)   (Loss of spatial details)    (Pure semantic abstraction)
                                                         │
                                                         ▼
                                               [ Language Backbone ]
                                                         │
                                                         ▼
                                               [ Discrete / Flow Action Head ]

  Problem: Physical manipulation requires metric 3D boundaries and depth,
           which were systematically discarded by the final encoder layer!
```

**One sentence**: The authors introduce **NEO-VLA**, a structurally **encoder-free** Vision-Language-Action architecture where pixels are directly embedded into the language backbone via lightweight patch convolutions, allowing a flow-matching action expert to tap into instruction-conditioned visual representations across multiple computational depths where action-relevant signals naturally peak.

## Core Framework & Mechanisms

NEO-VLA replaces the conventional multi-component vision-language-action pipeline with a unified, end-to-end framework:

### 1. Structurally Native Visual Interface
- **Zero Pretrained Vision Encoder**: NEO-VLA contains no external Vision Transformer (ViT), vector-quantized codebook, or autoencoder.
- **Direct Patch Embedding**: Raw images (up to 3 camera views per timestep: exterior, wrist, auxiliary) are dynamically tokenized using lightweight patch convolutional layers shared across views and time. Images preserve their native aspect ratios under a dynamic budget (up to 88 visual tokens per view).
- **Geometric and Proprioceptive Prefix**: Camera intrinsics, full 6-DoF poses, and robot joint states (Arm 0, Arm 1, mobile body) are mapped via lightweight MLPs and prepended alongside structured language instructions into a single unified sequence.

### 2. Action Readout Across Computational Depths
Where do action-relevant features actually live inside a foundation model? The authors performed layer-wise perturbation and swapping interventions:
- **Spatial Geometry Peaks Early**: Interventions on cached key-values reveal that end-effector spatial translation errors are most sensitive to layers **0–10**. Masking wrist camera representations at layers 6–10 causes immediate manipulation failure.
- **Instruction Grounding Peaks Mid-Network**: Language conditioning sensitivity exhibits strong peaks at layer 0 and layer 17.
- **The Multi-Depth Policy Map**: Instead of binding solely to the final layer, NEO-VLA's action expert reads cached Keys ($K$) and Values ($V$) from a distributed set of backbone layers across the computational hierarchy, granting the policy direct access to both low-level spatial geometry and high-level semantic intent.

### 3. Continuous Flow-Matching Action Expert
- The action expert is a narrow, randomly initialized Transformer trained via conditional flow matching.
- It receives projected noisy action tokens and flow time $\tau$, equipped with shared temporal rotary embeddings (t-RoPE) spanning execution horizons of 30 steps.
- The expert outputs a continuous velocity field covering 26-DoF dual-arm trajectories (joint angles, $\Delta$position, $\Delta$rotation, gripper states) and 8-DoF mobile base/torso coordinates.

## Practical Implications

1. **Retiring Separate Vision Encoders in Robotics**: The assumption that robotics requires frozen visual encoders pretrained on static image datasets is flawed. Native patch tokenization allows the network to preserve metric geometric structures, delivering higher control precision at lower parameter counts.
2. **Action-Critical Signals Peak Early**: Roboticists designing policy heads should not simply tap the final layer of a vision model. Cross-attending into early- and mid-level representations is necessary to preserve 3D boundaries and contact points.
3. **Multimodal Co-Training is Mandatory**: The paper demonstrates that training purely on action tokens degrades general visual comprehension and causes policy robustness to collapse under scene randomization (falling from 69.4% to 10.7% on RoboTwin C2R). Retaining general multimodal understanding data during policy tuning is essential for real-world generalization.

## 问题背景与核心痛点（The Gap）

当前主流的视觉-语言-动作（Vision-Language-Action, VLA）具身大模型范式（如 OpenVLA、Octo、$\pi_0$ 等）均建立在一个不言自明的架构假设之上：**原始视觉输入必须首先通过一个独立预训练的冻结视觉编码器（如 CLIP、SigLIP 或 DINOv2）完成感知压缩，随后语言基座与动作决策头仅在这一视觉“端点表征”之上进行下游计算**。

然而，物理世界的机器人操作与互联网图文理解存在着本质鸿沟：
1. **表征目标的根本错位**：传统视觉编码器针对互联网图像的“语义不变性”进行优化（例如：不论光照变化、距离远近或视角偏转，茶杯都被抽象为统一的“茶杯”语义）。然而，灵巧机器人操作严重依赖的恰恰是那些在语义抽象中被滤掉的底层空间信号：毫米级的绝对深度、接触面几何边缘、微小的滑移与相对姿态变换。
2. **深层抽象抹杀空间几何**：预训练视觉编码器在越靠近顶部的网络层，空间分辨率与几何保真度衰减越严重。当 VLA 模型直接读取编码器最顶层的抽象输出时，机器人完成接触式操作所需的高频空间几何细节早已荡然无存。
3. **架构层面的不可逆损失**：由于编码器作为刚性前置黑盒存在，下游语言模型无论如何放大参数规模或精调动作头，都无法“无中生有”还原在上游就被丢弃的几何线索。

```
                     传统编码器驱动 VLA 架构的根本缺陷

  原始相机像素输入
        │
        ▼
  [ 独立预训练视觉编码器 (CLIP / SigLIP / DINOv2) ]
  第 1 层 ──> 第 6 层 ──> 第 12 层 ──> 第 24 层 (编码器最终输出)
  (丰富3D几何与边缘)   (高频细节逐渐丢失)   (高度抽象的分类语义)
                                                   │
                                                   ▼
                                         [ 语言基座模型 LLM ]
                                                   │
                                                   ▼
                                         [ 离散 / 连续动作生成头 ]

  核心矛盾：机械臂抓取所需的局部精确几何与深度信息，
           早在进入大模型之前就被编码器的最终层彻底过滤！
```

## 关键机制与架构解析（Core Framework & Mechanism）

NEO-VLA 彻底重构了图文动作交互通路，构建了三大核心技术支柱：

### 1. 结构化原生视觉接口（Structurally Native）
- **彻底去除前置视觉编码器**：模型完全不依赖外部 ViT、离散 VQ 代码本或图像自编码器。
- **共享轻量卷积分块映射**：对于多视角相机输入（外部视角、腕部视角、辅助视角），图像通过时间与视角共享的轻量卷积层直接投射为语言基座的 Token 序列，动态保留长宽比，单视角仅消耗最多 88 个 Token。
- **几何与本体感知前缀融合**：相机焦距、主点、外参位姿以及双臂与底盘的关节状态，均通过专门的轻量 MLP 投射为结构化 Token，与语言控制指令一并输入基座。

### 2. 跨计算深度的动作特征探针与读取
机器人操作的关键特征到底分布在模型的哪一层？作者设计了严密的层级置换与掩码干预实验：
- **空间几何信号集中在中浅层**：对各层缓存的 Key-Value 进行替换干预发现，末端执行器的平移误差对**第 0–10 层**极其敏感。对第 6–10 层的腕部相机 Token 进行掩码会导致机械臂操作彻底失败；
- **指令对齐呈现双峰分布**：语言指令的引导信号在第 0 层与第 17 层出现显著峰值；
- **多深度特征映射策略（Layer-Map）**：区别于传统方案只读取最后一层的做法，NEO-VLA 的动作专家直接从基座的多层计算节点（早、中、晚期各层）并行提取 Key-Value，使策略既能直接感知浅层的绝对几何结构，又能结合深层的高阶语义上下文。

### 3. 基于条件流匹配（Flow Matching）的连续动作生成
- 动作专家设计为一个随机初始化的紧凑 Transformer，通过条件流匹配（Flow Matching）拟合连续动作场。
- 支持 30 步动作块（Action Chunking）并发预测，使用共享的时序旋转位置编码（t-RoPE）。
- 单次前向同时输出双臂 26 维关节/位姿增量以及底盘和躯干的 8 维全身协同动作。

## 实践启示与行业影响（Practical Implications）

1. **告别外挂视觉编码器**：机器人控制并不适合照搬用于图文匹配的预训练 ViT 编码器。采用原生卷积分块将像素直连语言基座，能够从根本上保留三维度量几何与接触信息。
2. **动作头设计应跳出“只读最后一层”的误区**：具身策略动作头应当被设计为能够跨层访问浅层与中层网络激活，从而同时捕获中浅层的空间几何信息与深层的抽象时序意图。
3. **具身模型后训练必须坚持多模态联合训练**：实验表明，如果后训练阶段完全摒弃多模态理解数据而仅使用纯动作数据微调，会导致模型的场景泛化能力崩溃（RoboTwin 泛化得分从 69.4% 骤降至 10.7%）。保留多模态图文理解能力是实现鲁棒泛化控制的必要前提。
