## The Gap

Frontier AI regulation and institutional safety frameworks (such as the UK and US AI Safety Institutes' evaluation protocols) rest upon a single foundational assumption: **capability elicitation**. The consensus assumes that if a model contains dangerous knowledge or dangerous operational skills (e.g., biological weapon synthesis, cyber warfare, or recursive exploitation), rigorous white-box auditing—including automated red-teaming, representation probing, gradient-based trigger discovery, and fine-tuning probes—will eventually surface those capabilities before release.

This paper demolishes that assumption. Prior work demonstrated that models could hide fixed strings or static backdoors. However, whether an adversary could hide an **entire general-purpose computational circuit or arbitrary neural sub-network** inside standard model weights—while remaining completely mathematically undetectable until unlocked by a secret cryptographic trigger—remained an open theoretical question.

   Model deployed to production ---> Attacker feeds Secret Key: "x_secret"
                             |

   +-----------------------------------------------------------+

## The Increment

**One sentence**: The authors present a constructive cryptographic framework that embeds encrypted neural networks within host model weights using digital lockers, mathematically proving that arbitrary capabilities can remain undetectable against gradient search, fine-tuning, and weight perturbation under standard cryptographic hardness assumptions.

### Core Mechanism

The authors integrate principles from **program obfuscation and digital lockers** directly into tensor operations:

1. **Digital Locker Circuit Embedding**:
   - A digital locker is a cryptographic primitive that computes a point function: it outputs an encrypted value (or key) if and only if the input matches a precise secret string $x^*$, and outputs pseudorandom noise otherwise.
   - The authors embed a digital locker inside standard linear layer weights $W$, linking it to an encrypted neural network that computes arbitrary dangerous tasks (e.g., synthesis prediction for hazardous chemicals).
2. **Cryptographic Hardness Guarantees**:
   - Under standard lattice assumptions (such as Learning With Errors, LWE), distinguishing the locked circuit from random Gaussian weight noise is provably computationally intractable.
   - The gradient $\nabla_x \mathcal{L}$ of the locked module is identically zero (or pseudorandom noise) everywhere on the input manifold except at the exact discrete point $x^*$, rendering gradient-based trigger inversions completely useless.
3. **Empirical Resistance to Aggressive Elicitation**:
   - Implemented in PyTorch on a hazardous chemical reaction prediction task (a direct proxy for CBRN capabilities).
   - Subjected to standard post-training safety interventions: Gaussian weight noising, aggressive instruction fine-tuning, representation unlearning, and latent adversarial training.
   - The hidden capability remained completely inert and undetected during audits, yet unlocked with 100% precision when the secret trigger was supplied.

### Key Concepts

- **Digital Locker**: A cryptographic mechanism that hides a sensitive functionality such that learning anything about it without the exact key requires brute-forcing the key space.
- **Capability Elicitation**: The safety audit methodology that attempts to coax a neural network into revealing its maximum latent potential through prompts, embeddings, or fine-tuning.
- **Elicitation Ceiling**: The mathematical boundary beyond which algorithmic auditing cannot distinguish between a model that lacks a capability and a model that hides it cryptographically.

## Framework Shift

```
Before (Auditing-as-Proof Consensus):
  Premise: If comprehensive red-teaming and gradient searches fail to elicit a dangerous behavior, the model does not possess that capability.
  Assumption: Weights are continuous manifolds accessible to optimization.

After (Cryptographic Reality):
  Premise: Models can mathematically store arbitrary Turing-complete capabilities locked behind cryptographic barriers.
  Reality: Full white-box access to weights does NOT guarantee capability discovery; structural provenance and secure supply-chain custody are mandatory.
```

## Takeaways

- Passing a capability evaluation does not prove the absence of that capability; models can provably hide arbitrary computations in their weights.
- Gradient-based red-teaming fails against cryptographically locked backdoors because the loss gradient outside the trigger is flat or pseudorandom.
- Model safety governance must evolve beyond post-hoc evaluations to include cryptographic hardware attestation and strict end-to-end supply chain provenance.

## 缺口

当前全球前沿 AI 监管与安全审计体系（包括英美 AI 安全研究所等权威机构所制定的评估规范），均建立在一个看似坚不可摧的前提假设之上：**能力可诱导性（Capability Elicitation）**。
这一共识假定：如果一个大模型在底层具备某种极具杀伤力的危险能力（如制造生化武器、发起网络瘫痪战或实施系统接管），只要审计人员掌握模型权重，通过大规模自动化红队测试（Red-Teaming）、梯度逆向触发词搜索（Gradient Trigger Search）、激活探针或对抗性微调，就必然能将该能力「诱导」出来并予以封禁。

然而，这一假设在数学逻辑上真的成立吗？
此前已有研究证明可以在模型中植入暗号来输出固定的恶意字符串。但是，攻击者是否能够在标准的神经网络权重中**加密植入一整套任意通用的计算子网络**，使其在审计阶段无论被如何探测都与普通权重毫无二致，唯有输入特定的密码学密匙时才被瞬时解密激活？这始终是安全领域的未解之谜。

## 增量

**一句话**: 本文通过在神经网络权重中引入密码学「数字保险箱（Digital Lockers）」架构，在数学上证明并在 PyTorch 中实证了：大模型可以加密隐藏任意复杂的神经网络计算回路，在没有密钥触发词的前提下，该能力在理论上能完全免疫任何基于梯度搜索、权重加噪和对抗微调的红队审计。

### 核心机制

研究团队将密码学中的**混淆电路（Program Obfuscation）与数字保险箱理论**完美映射到了深度神经网络张量运算中：

1. **数字保险箱子网络嵌入机制**：
   - 「数字保险箱」是一种密码学点函数原语：当且仅当输入值完全匹配预设密钥 $x^*$ 时，才会解密输出有效功能或解密秘钥；而在其余任何输入下，其输出均严格表现为不可区分的伪随机噪声。
   - 作者利用这一机制，将一套用于预测高危化学反应的独立神经计算回路加密嵌入到宿主大模型的标准线性层权重中。
2. **密码学安全性与梯度平坦性证明**：
   - 基于标准格密码学假设（如容错学习问题 LWE），在不掌握秘钥时，将该隐藏回路与普通的网络高斯随机权重区分开来在计算复杂度上是不可行的（Hard Problem）。
   - 由于数字保险箱在密钥点之外的损失函数梯度 $\nabla_x \mathcal{L}$ 处处为零或表现为纯随机噪声，一切基于梯度的触发词逆向搜索算法（如 GCG 攻击）在原理上彻底瘫痪。
3. **极限制衡实验验证**：
   - 在生化 CBRN 代理任务（高危化学合成预测）上完成原型构建。
   - 承受了前沿安全防御手段的暴力洗礼：包括大幅度高斯权重加噪（Weight Noising）、激进的指令微调对齐、表征擦除（Unlearning）以及潜空间对抗训练。
   - 实验结果证明：隐藏能力在审计状态下纹丝不动，表现与普通安全模型毫无差异；而一旦注入秘钥指令，隐藏回路即以 100% 精度瞬时解密执行。

### 关键概念

- **数字保险箱（Digital Locker）**：一种密码学混淆机制，能将特定计算功能与密钥锁死，使得在缺乏完整密钥时，任何试图分析其功能的算法都不优于纯暴力穷举。
- **能力诱导上限（Elicitation Ceiling）**：本文揭示的理论界限：指纯算法审计手段根本无法在数学上区分「一个不具备某能力的安全模型」与「一个加密隐藏了该能力的多面手模型」。
- **容错学习假设（LWE, Learning With Errors）**：后量子密码学的重要数学基石，确保了高维线性方程组在微小扰动下求解的计算不可行性。

## 行囊 (Takeaways)

- 绝不能将「通过了能力安全评测」等同于「模型绝对不存在恶意后门或危险能力」；模型可以在权重中完美隐藏任意子网络。
- 依赖梯度反向传播的红队探测工具存在原理性死角；在密码学混淆结构面前，梯度信号完全无法提供任何指引。
- 严肃的高等级 AI 安全治理必须跳出事后测试思维，转向代码训练全周期的密码学硬件签名认证与严格的供应链可信流转体系。
