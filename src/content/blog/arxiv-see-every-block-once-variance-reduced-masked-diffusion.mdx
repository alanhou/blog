## The Gap

Masked Diffusion Language Models (MDLMs) are emerging as high-speed, non-autoregressive alternatives to standard autoregressive transformers. By casting sequence generation as iteratively unmasking tokens via an absorbing-state diffusion process, MDLMs can generate text in flexible, non-left-to-right orders. To achieve high generative coherence, modern systems partition sequences of length $L$ into $C$ sequential blocks of size $B$ ($L = B \times C$).

However, aligning MDLMs with human preferences via reinforcement learning (RL) has been plagued by severe optimization instability:
- Unlike autoregressive models where policy gradients use exact token log-probabilities $\sum \log p(y_t | y_{<t})$, discrete diffusion log-likelihoods are intractable.
- Post-training must rely on a Monte Carlo surrogate of the Evidence Lower Bound (ELBO): $\mathcal{L}_{\text{Block}}$.
- Current post-training algorithms (such as SPG and random block baselines) estimate this surrogate by uniformly sampling random block indices $b_k \sim \text{Unif}(\\{{0, \dots, C-1\\}})$ across $K$ Monte Carlo trials.

When the number of blocks is modest ($C \in \\{{2, 4, 8\\}}$) and sample budget is constrained ($K = C = 4$), random sampling causes catastrophic **block miss rates**: in 91% of trials, at least one entire block is never sampled, while others are sampled redundantly. This induces massive **between-group variance** $\sigma^2_{\text{between}}$ across blocks, injecting severe gradient noise that paralyzes policy gradient updates.

## The Increment

**One sentence**: By replacing random block index sampling with a deterministic full-block estimator that evaluates every block exactly once, this paper mathematically eliminates between-group block-selection variance while preserving unbiasedness, delivering an average +12.79 point accuracy gain across five reasoning benchmarks.

### Core Mechanism

Consider an output sequence of $L$ tokens divided into $C = L/B$ blocks. In blockwise generation, when generating block $b$, all prior blocks ($b' < b$) are conditioned upon as fixed context, within-block tokens are masked with ratio $t_b \in (0, 1]$, and all downstream blocks ($b' > b$) remain fully masked.

The true blockwise surrogate objective is:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{Block}}(o | c; \theta) = \mathbb{E}_{b, t_b, M_{t_b}} [\ell_{b, t_b, M_{t_b}}(o, c; \theta)]$$

Existing methods compute a Monte Carlo estimate with $K$ random draws:
$$\hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{Rand}} = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=1}^K \ell_{b_k, t_k, M_k}, \quad b_k \sim \text{Unif}(\\{{0, \dots, C-1\\}})$$

Using the law of total variance, the variance of this random estimator decomposes into:
$$\text{Var}[\hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{Rand}}] = \frac{1}{K} \left( \sigma^2_{\text{between}} + \bar{\sigma}^2_{\text{within}} \right)$$
where $\sigma^2_{\text{between}} = \frac{1}{C}\sum_{b=0}^{C-1} (\mu_b - \bar{\mu})^2$ measures the discrepancy in loss magnitudes across different blocks (e.g., initial reasoning steps vs. final answers), and $\bar{\sigma}^2_{\text{within}}$ is the mean intra-block stochastic masking variance.

The authors propose the **Full-Block Estimator** $\hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{Full}}$:
$$\hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{Full}}(o | c; \theta) = \frac{1}{C} \sum_{b=0}^{C-1} \ell_{b, t_b, M_{t_b}}(o, c; \theta)$$
where each block $b \in \\{{0, \dots, C-1\\}}$ is visited **deterministically and exactly once**, while masking ratios $t_b$ and mask sets $M_{t_b}$ remain independently sampled per block.

Because the block index is no longer a random variable:
$$\text{Var}[\hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{Full}}] = \frac{\bar{\sigma}^2_{\text{within}}}{C}$$
The between-group variance $\sigma^2_{\text{between}}$ is **identically zero**. At matched sample size $K = C$:
$$\frac{\text{Var}[\hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{Full}}]}{\text{Var}[\hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{Rand}}]} = \frac{\bar{\sigma}^2_{\text{within}}}{\sigma^2_{\text{between}} + \bar{\sigma}^2_{\text{within}}} \le 1$$

```
Random Sampling (Baseline, K=4):
  Draw 1: Block 0  |  Draw 2: Block 2  |  Draw 3: Block 0  |  Draw 4: Block 2
  Result: Block 1 and Block 3 are completely ignored!
  Gradient noise: High between-block error sigma^2_between corrupts update.

Full-Block Estimator (SEBO, C=4):
  Step 0: Block 0  |  Step 1: Block 1  |  Step 2: Block 2  |  Step 3: Block 3
  Result: Every block evaluated exactly once.
  Gradient noise: sigma^2_between is mathematically wiped to 0. Unbiased.
```

**Structural Metaphor**: Imagine a health inspector auditing a 4-station commercial kitchen (Prep, Grill, Fryer, Dishwashing) with a budget of 4 checks. A careless inspector (random sampling) rolls a 4-sided die four times and ends up checking Prep twice and Grill twice, completely missing the Fryer and Dishwashing stations. If the uninspected Dishwashing station has a severe leak, the audit report is wildly unrepresentative of the kitchen's true hygiene. In contrast, the methodical inspector (SEBO) walks through every single station in sequence, spending one check on Prep, one on Grill, one on Fryer, and one on Dishwashing. The inspection covers the exact same number of checks (4), but station-selection variance is eliminated, giving a reliable, unbiased, and vastly more accurate appraisal of kitchen quality.

### Key Concepts

- **Absorbing-State Diffusion**: A discrete diffusion formulation where tokens transition irreversibly into a special mask token $\textsf{M}$ during the forward process, rather than adding Gaussian noise to continuous vectors.
- **Between-Group vs. Within-Group Variance**: In stratified statistics, total variance splits into variance *between* strata (differences in expected loss across sequence blocks) and variance *within* strata (randomness in token masking positions for a given block). SEBO annihilates between-strata variance by exhaustive stratification.
- **Block Miss Rate**: The probability $1 - \frac{C! S_2(K, C)}{C^K}$ (where $S_2$ is the Stirling number of the second kind) that at least one block receives zero evaluations under $K$ random draws. At $K=C=4$, this failure probability is 91.4%.

## 缺口

掩码扩散语言模型（Masked Diffusion Language Models, MDLMs）凭借非自回归的并行解码能力与全局上下文双向注意机制，正在成为挑战传统自回归 Transformer 的核心力量。
为了在长文本生成中兼顾全局规划与局部连贯性，前沿架构普遍采用**分块生成策略（Blockwise Generation）**，将长度为 $L$ 的序列划分为 $C$ 个长度为 $B$ 的语义块（$L = B \times C$）。

然而，当利用强化学习（RL，如 GRPO/PPO）对 MDLM 进行人类偏好对齐后训练时，研究者遭遇了极度严重的训练不稳定与策略梯度崩溃：
- 与自回归模型能够精确计算逐 Token 对数似然 $\sum \log p(y_t | y_{<t})$ 不同，离散扩散模型的边际似然在计算上是不可解析的。
- 强化学习必须依赖于基于证据下界（ELBO）的分块代理目标函数 $\mathcal{L}_{\text{Block}}$。
- 现有算法（包括 Meta 的 SPG 以及主流随机分块基线）在计算该代理损失时，采用蒙特卡洛随机采样：在 $K$ 次采样中独立均匀地抽取分块索引 $b_k \sim \text{Unif}(\\{{0, \dots, C-1\\}})$。

当序列分块数较小（如 $C \in \\{{2, 4, 8\\}}$）且计算预算受限（$K = C = 4$）时，随机采样会导致灾难性的**分块漏检（Block Miss）**：
数学证明在 $K=C=4$ 时，**至少有一个完整分块完全未被采样的概率高达 91%**，而其他分块则被重复评估。
这种采样偏差在分块之间引入了巨大的**组间方差（Between-Group Variance）** $\sigma^2_{\text{between}}$，向策略梯度注入了剧烈的随机噪声，直接导致强化学习震荡与性能劣化。

## 增量

**一句话**: 本文通过提出确定性遍历所有分块且仅评估一次的全块估计器（SEBO），在严格保持目标无偏性的前提下数学消除组间采样方差，使 MDLM 在五大推理基准上的平均精度提升 12.79 分。

### 核心机制

设模型生成的输出序列包含 $L$ 个 Token，划分为 $C = L/B$ 个分块。
在分块生成过程中，评估第 $b$ 块时，前序分块（$b' < b$）作为已知上下文全保留，当前块按时间步 $t_b \in (0, 1]$ 掩码部分 Token，而后序未生成分块（$b' > b$）则全部保持掩码状态。

真实的分块代理目标函数为：
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{Block}}(o | c; \theta) = \mathbb{E}_{b, t_b, M_{t_b}} [\ell_{b, t_b, M_{t_b}}(o, c; \theta)]$$

现有方法采用 $K$ 次独立同分布的随机蒙特卡洛抽样进行估计：
$$\hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{Rand}} = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=1}^K \ell_{b_k, t_k, M_k}, \quad b_k \sim \text{Unif}(\\{{0, \dots, C-1\\}})$$

根据全方差定律（Law of Total Variance），每个采样的总方差分解为：
$$\text{Var}[\hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{Rand}}] = \frac{1}{K} \left( \sigma^2_{\text{between}} + \bar{\sigma}^2_{\text{within}} \right)$$
其中组间方差 $\sigma^2_{\text{between}} = \frac{1}{C}\sum_{b=0}^{C-1} (\mu_b - \bar{\mu})^2$ 刻画了不同位置分块（如起步推理与最终结论）损失期望的内在离散度；而 $\bar{\sigma}^2_{\text{within}}$ 为块内随机掩码位置带来的方差均值。

作者提出的**全块估计器（Full-Block Estimator, SEBO）**定义为：
$$\hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{Full}}(o | c; \theta) = \frac{1}{C} \sum_{b=0}^{C-1} \ell_{b, t_b, M_{t_b}}(o, c; \theta)$$
这里分块索引 $b$ **完全确定性地遍历 $\\{{0, \dots, C-1\\}}$ 中的每一个数值且仅出现一次**，各块内的掩码比例 $t_b$ 与掩码子集 $M_{t_b}$ 依然保持块间独立采样。

由于分块索引不再具备随机性，其方差坍缩为：
$$\text{Var}[\hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{Full}}] = \frac{\bar{\sigma}^2_{\text{within}}}{C}$$
组间方差 $\sigma^2_{\text{between}}$ 被**严格归零**。
在相同评估预算 $K = C$ 下，方差比值为：
$$\frac{\text{Var}[\hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{Full}}]}{\text{Var}[\hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{Rand}}]} = \frac{\bar{\sigma}^2_{\text{within}}}{\sigma^2_{\text{between}} + \bar{\sigma}^2_{\text{within}}} \le 1$$

```
随机抽样基线（K = 4 采样）：
  抽样 1: 块 0  |  抽样 2: 块 2  |  抽样 3: 块 0  |  抽样 4: 块 2
  结果：块 1 和 块 3 完全被遗漏！
  梯度噪声：巨大的组间波动 sigma^2_between 污染参数更新。

全块估计器 SEBO（C = 4 确定性遍历）：
  评估 0: 块 0  |  评估 1: 块 1  |  评估 2: 块 2  |  评估 3: 块 3
  结果：每个分块不偏不倚全部覆盖。
  梯度噪声：组间方差数学归零，目标严格保持无偏。
```

**核喻（后厨卫生四岗位巡检）**：
将序列分块评估比作**食品安全检查员对包含 4 个工作区（备菜区、烤架区、油炸区、洗碗区）的大型后厨进行抽样检查**。

### 关键概念

- **吸收态扩散过程（Absorbing-State Diffusion）**：离散扩散模型的前向加噪过程，序列中的原始词元不加连续高斯噪声，而是按照概率不可逆地替换为特殊的掩码符号 $\textsf{M}$。
- **组间方差与组内方差（Between vs. Within Variance）**：分层统计学概念。
组间方差衡量不同文本位置（如前缀、思维主干、结论）本身损失难度的剧烈差异；组内方差衡量相同位置内部不同掩码组合的随机扰动。
SEBO 巧妙地通过完备分层彻底清零了组间方差。
- **分块遗漏概率（Block Miss Probability）**：在独立均匀采样 $K$ 次且分块数为 $C$ 时，至少漏掉一个块的概率公式为 $1 - \frac{C! S_2(K, C)}{C^K}$（其中 $S_2$ 为第二类斯特林数）。
当 $K=C=4$ 时，漏检率高达惊人的 91.4%。
