## The Gap

Scaling generalist robot manipulation policies—such as Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and World Action Models (WAMs)—is bottlenecked by the extreme scarcity of robot teleoperation data. Collecting real-world robot demonstrations is economically prohibitive, slow, and inherently fragmented: Franka arms, UR5 manipulators, ALOHA bimanual setups, and humanoid robots all possess fundamentally different joint configurations, kinematics, and action control spaces.

First-person human videos (such as Ego4D, EgoDex, and EgoVerse) offer an inexhaustible, highly diverse stream of manipulation skills. However, **human videos lack robot action labels**, creating a severe embodiment and domain gap.

Prior Latent Action Models (LAMs, such as Genie, LAPA, and MoTo) attempted to bypass this mismatch by learning discrete latent actions from adjacent frame transitions ($o_t \to o_{t+1}$). Yet, existing approaches suffer from three fatal flaws:
1. **Appearance Entanglement**: Because latent action encoders are trained from scratch on pixel reconstruction losses over isolated frame pairs, the learned representations inevitably entangle irrelevant domain visual features—such as human arm skin tones, camera viewpoints, background textures, and table colors—with the actual physical motion.
2. **Short-Horizon Myopia**: Encoders operating solely over two consecutive frames fail to capture temporal continuity, causal dynamics, and multi-step manipulation intent.
3. **Cross-Embodiment Failure**: Latent actions learned on human demonstrations fail to zero-shot transfer onto real robot hardware without extensive, expensive paired alignment data.

```
                  THE CROSS-EMBODIMENT DATA BOTTLENECK

  Abundant Internet Data:                Physical Robot Deployments:
  [Human Egocentric Videos]               [Franka / UR5 / ALOHA / Humanoids]
  - Massive diversity & volume            - Extremely expensive teleoperation
  - NO robot action labels                - Fragmented joint & control spaces
  - Human arm morphology                  - Custom gripper physics
             │                                       │
             ▼                                       ▼
  Prior LAMs (Genie, LAPA):
  Short-frame encoder ($o_t \to o_{t+1}$) ──> Entangles background, viewpoint & skin!
  Zero-shot transfer to robot fails catastrophically.
```

## Core Framework & Mechanisms

DreamX rearchitects the pretraining and inference pipeline of generative video diffusion models into a unified representation learner for physical dynamics:

### 1. Latent Action Encoding via Learnable Spatiotemporal Queries
Instead of training a separate convolutional or transformer encoder from scratch, DreamX directly taps into the internal spatiotemporal representations of a pretrained video diffusion backbone (**Cosmos-Predict 2.5 2B**):
- For each latent video frame group $V_g \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times D}$ (consisting of 1,200 video tokens), DreamX appends a compact set of $K = 4$ learnable query tokens $q_g$.
- The query tokens pass through the backbone's cross-attention and self-attention layers alongside the video tokens.
- **Frame-Causal Masking**: Queries $q_g$ attend only to current and past video tokens $V_{0:g}$ in a strictly causal manner, while query-to-query attention is bidirectional across the sequence.
- The encoder outputs the compact latent action sequence:
  $$Q = \mathcal{E}_\phi(q; V, \ell)$$
  where $\ell$ denotes text task instructions. This achieves an unprecedented **300x compression ratio** ($1,200 \to 4$ tokens).

### 2. Generative Decoding Disentangles Motion from Appearance
Why does DreamX avoid entangling background appearance and embodiment specifics?
- The decoder $\mathcal{D}_\theta$ is conditioned strictly on the **initial observation frame** $V_0$ and the compact latent queries $Q$, without text guidance.
- Because $V_0$ already provides the scene's appearance, camera angle, and static morphology, and the pretrained video diffusion model already possesses a world-model prior over natural scene physics, **the latent action queries $Q$ are forced solely to carry the residual motion dynamics** needed to reconstruct future frames $\hat{V}_{1:T}$.
- An asymmetric attention mask prevents decoder video and action tokens from attending to each other, forcing all dynamic supervision through the latent action bottleneck.

### 3. Dual Cross-Embodiment Transfer
Once pre-trained on diverse robot demonstrations (Bridge, DROID, AgiBot, MolmoAct2) and human videos (EgoDex, EgoVerse), the extracted latent representation $Q_{\text{src}}$ functions as a universal action lingua franca:
1. **Video Transfer**: Combining $Q_{\text{src}}$ with a target initial frame $V_0^{\text{tgt}}$ yields a target video reproducing the source motion under a completely different embodiment and scene:
   $$\hat{V}^{\text{tgt}} = \mathcal{D}_\theta(Q_{\text{src}}, V_0^{\text{tgt}})$$
2. **Zero-Shot Action Transfer**: Combining $Q_{\text{src}}$ with target robot proprioceptive state $p_0^{\text{tgt}}$ decodes the human demonstration directly into continuous, executable robot joint actions without any paired demonstration data:
   $$\hat{A}^{\text{tgt}} = \mathcal{D}_\theta(Q_{\text{src}}, p_0^{\text{tgt}})$$

### 4. Policy Learning in Compact Latent Space
Rather than executing compute-heavy video diffusion rollouts at inference time, DreamX trains a lightweight latent policy $\pi(Z, A \mid V_0, \ell, p_0)$:
- Unlabeled human videos supervise latent code prediction ($Z$) via $\mathcal{L}_{\text{latent}}$.
- Robot demonstrations supervise low-level action prediction ($A$) via a dedicated Diffusion Transformer (DiT) head.
- This decoupled policy architecture cuts inference compute by **over 80%** compared to full video-diffusion policies.

## Practical Implications

1. **Unlocking Web-Scale Human Video for Robotics**: DreamX eliminates the need to collect billions of dollars in teleoperated robot data. By learning a shared latent action space invariant to visual appearance and embodiment, any first-person video from YouTube or everyday wearable cameras can directly train robotic manipulation policies.
2. **Solving Embodiment Fragmentation**: Robotics has suffered from incompatible action spaces across different hardware vendors. DreamX's 4-token latent action acts as a universal intermediate interface that any robot arm can execute via a local proprioceptive decoder.
3. **High-Throughput World Action Models**: By compressing diffusion tokens by 300x, DreamX enables real-time, low-latency world model planning on edge compute hardware, overcoming the prohibitive inference latency of standard diffusion rollouts.

## 问题背景与核心痛点（The Gap）

训练通用机器人大模型（如视觉-语言-动作 VLA 模型与世界动作模型 WAM）面临的核心壁垒是**真实机器人遥操作数据的极度稀缺与高昂成本**。在物理世界中采集机器人示教数据不仅耗资巨大，而且存在严重的硬件割裂：Franka 机械臂、UR5、双臂 ALOHA 以及各类人形机器人的关节自由度、运动学构型和控制动作空间截然不同，彼此数据难以直接互通。

第一人称人类操作视频（如 Ego4D、EgoDex 与 EgoVerse）提供了几乎取之不尽、涵盖海量交互技能的视觉宝库。然而，**人类视频完全缺乏机器人的底层关节动作标签**，存在巨大的具身构型鸿沟（Embodiment Gap）。

现有的隐式动作模型（Latent Action Models, LAMs，如 Genie、LAPA、MoTo 等）试图通过相邻帧之间的视觉变化（$o_t \to o_{t+1}$）自监督学习离散动作表征。但现有方案存在三大致命瓶颈：
1. **动作与外观严重纠缠（Appearance Entanglement）**：由于现存编码器从零开始基于像素重构损失进行训练，学到的隐式表示不可避免地纠缠了环境的背景纹理、拍摄视角、桌面颜色以及人类手臂形态等与动作无关的视觉干扰。
2. **极短视界的局部盲区**：仅依赖双帧间的局部差异无法捕捉连续操作的时序动力学与多步任务意图。
3. **跨具身迁移彻底失效**：在人类视频上学到的隐式动作，由于无法剥离人类生物外观与特定场景特征，在没有大量成对对齐数据的情况下，根本无法直接驱动物理机器人执行任务。

## 关键机制与架构解析（Core Framework & Mechanism）

DreamX 将视频扩散生成模型的先验优势与机器人控制目标深度结合，构建了四大关键技术基石：

### 1. 基于可学习时空查询的隐式动作编码
DreamX 彻底抛弃了从零训练编码器的旧思路，直接复用预训练视频扩散基座（**Cosmos-Predict 2.5 2B**）内部丰富的时空特征：
- 针对视频潜层中的每个时序分组 $V_g \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times D}$（包含 1,200 个视觉 Token），DreamX 引入一组紧凑的 $K = 4$ 个可学习查询向量 $q_g$。
- 查询向量与视频 Token 一同送入骨干网络的自注意力与交叉注意力层。
- **帧级因果掩码设计**：查询向量 $q_g$ 严格以因果方式仅单向关注当前及历史帧的视觉 Token $V_{0:g}$，而查询向量彼此之间保持完全双向交互，精准捕捉动作的时序连贯性。
- 编码器最终前向输出紧凑的隐式动作序列：
  $$Q = \mathcal{E}_\phi(q; V, \ell)$$
  其中 $\ell$ 为语言任务指令。由此达成了惊人的 **300 倍时空压缩比**（$1,200 \to 4$ Tokens）。

### 2. 生成式解码实现动作与外观的完美解耦
为何 DreamX 能彻底剥离人类外观与环境杂质？
- 解码器 $\mathcal{D}_\theta$ 仅以**初始观察帧** $V_0$ 和紧凑隐式动作查询 $Q$ 为条件，不注入任何文本引导。
- 由于 $V_0$ 已经提供了场景的全部静态几何外观、材质、背景与物体构型，同时预训练视频扩散模型本身具备深厚的物理运动演化先验，**隐式动作 $Q$ 被严格倒逼仅承载解释未来状态演化所必需的纯运动动力学**，完全不需要浪费容量去编码已有的外观信息。
- 非对称注意力掩码确保解码器中的视频 Token 与动作 Token 均只能读取 $Q$，彼此之间严格互盲，杜绝了表征泄漏。

### 3. 双轨跨具身迁移机制
在混流预训练后，提取的纯净隐式动作 $Q_{\text{src}}$ 成为跨硬件的通用“动作世界语”：
1. **视频跨域迁移**：将 $Q_{\text{src}}$ 与任意目标场景的初始帧 $V_0^{\text{tgt}}$ 结合，解码器即可直接合成出目标场景与新机械臂复现该动作的高清视频：
   $$\hat{V}^{\text{tgt}} = \mathcal{D}_\theta(Q_{\text{src}}, V_0^{\text{tgt}})$$
2. **零样本动作直驱**：将人类视频中提取的 $Q_{\text{src}}$ 与目标机器人的本体状态 $p_0^{\text{tgt}}$ 结合，通过动作分支解码器直接生成连续、物理可执行的底层关节轨迹，**全程无需任何成对的人-机对齐数据**：
   $$\hat{A}^{\text{tgt}} = \mathcal{D}_\theta(Q_{\text{src}}, p_0^{\text{tgt}})$$

### 4. 紧凑隐式空间的轻量级策略部署
不同于在运行时反复执行极其耗时的全视频扩散推演，DreamX 构建了两阶段解耦策略 $\pi(Z, A \mid V_0, \ell, p_0)$：
- 海量互联网无标注人类视频专门用于监督紧凑隐式动作代码（$Z$）的高阶意图预测；
- 少量机器人标注数据仅用于监督专用的 DiT 动作头，将 $Z$ 映射为具体关节控制指令；
- 这使得真机推理时的计算开销较传统视频扩散策略降低 **80% 以上**。
